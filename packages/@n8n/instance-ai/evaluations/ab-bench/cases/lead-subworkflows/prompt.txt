When a new lead comes in through a webhook with name, email, company and message, clean it up, score it, save it in a data table called "leads", and reply to the caller with the score. Make the cleanup and the scoring two separate reusable workflows that my other workflows can call too. The cleanup trims and lowercases the email, title-cases the name, and takes the domain from the email. The scoring gives 0 to 100 points: 40 if the email domain is not gmail.com, yahoo.com or hotmail.com, 30 if a company is given, and 30 if the message is longer than 50 characters.
